OHIO 1980 Cold Case Solved After 24 Years — Justice for Sister Margaret Ann Pahl

On Holy Saturday, April 5, 1980, Sister Margaret Ann Pahl, a 71-year-old Sister of Mercy, went into the sacristy of St. Joseph's Chapel at Mercy Hospital in Toledo, Ohio, to prepare for the Easter services. She never came out. She had spent more than 52 years as a nun, as a nurse, a director of nursing and a hospital administrator, and in her last years she cared for the hospital's chapels. Within weeks, Toledo police had a suspect and an object that fit the description of the weapon. Then an interview was cut short, reports went missing, and the case went cold. For 24 years, an altar cloth collected at the scene sat in storage. In 2003, a complaint made to a church review board set off a chain of events that brought cold case investigators back to the file. What they found on that cloth was not DNA. It was a shape, a bloodstain transfer pattern, and it changed everything.

In this documentary: the life of Sister Margaret Ann, the 1980 investigation and why it stopped, the cold case team that reopened it, the bloodstain pattern evidence, the 2006 trial, the appeals, and the questions that remain.
