PENNSYLVANIA 1992 Cold Case Solved After 25 Years — Justice for Christy Mirack

On the morning of Monday, December 21, 1992, four days before Christmas, 25-year-old Christy Mirack, a sixth-grade teacher at Rohrerstown Elementary School, was getting ready to leave her townhouse in Greenfield Estates, East Lampeter Township, Lancaster County, Pennsylvania. Her roommate left around 7 a.m. Christy usually left at about 7:45. She never made it to school. Shortly after 9 a.m., her principal, Harry Goodman, found her. She had been beaten, sexually assaulted and strangled, still dressed in her coat and gloves. Investigators interviewed more than 500 people. DNA ruled out their main suspect and three other men. The FBI joined the case, but the DNA profile matched no one in CODIS. For 25 years, the case sat cold while Christy's family waited. Then Lancaster County detectives took it over and turned to Parabon NanoLabs, first for DNA-based composite portraits and then for genetic genealogy. In 2018, undercover investigators followed one man to an elementary school party and waited for him to throw away a water bottle and a piece of chewing gum.

In this documentary: who Christy was, the morning she was killed, the 25-year investigation, the genetic genealogy breakthrough, and the court case that followed.
