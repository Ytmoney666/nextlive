MICHIGAN 1987 Cold Case Solved After 26 Years — Justice for Rick & Gail Brink

On Monday, November 23, 1987, Rick Brink, 28, and his wife Gail, 22, did not show up for work. They had been married about eighteen months and had just bought a ranch house in Park Township, near Holland, Michigan. Rick's family drove to the house. Rick was found in the driver's seat of his Chevy Blazer in the driveway. Gail was found inside. Both had been shot with a .22 caliber pistol. It was three days before Thanksgiving. Detectives interviewed the people closest to the couple within days, checked their alibis and made no arrest. For more than twenty-five years the case stayed cold, while a family secret stayed buried. Then, in 2011, two Ottawa County cold case detectives reopened thousands of pages of old files, conducted more than 200 interviews, and noticed that one old story did not match itself. No DNA miracle and no genealogy tree: this case was solved by reading, re-interviewing and a witness who finally spoke.

In this documentary: who Rick and Gail Brink were, the weekend they died, the alibi that held for 25 years, how two detectives cracked the case, the 2014 trial and sentence, and the appeals that followed.
