CALIFORNIA 1996 Cold Case Solved After 26 Years — Justice for Kristin Smart

At about 2 a.m. on Saturday, May 25, 1996, Memorial Day weekend, 19-year-old Kristin Smart, a Cal Poly freshman from Stockton, California, was heading back toward the dorms in San Luis Obispo after an off-campus party on Crandall Way. She had been drinking heavily and could barely walk. Two fellow students walked with her. One of them split off near Perimeter Road and Grand Avenue. Kristin never reached her dorm. Campus police did not open a report when her disappearance was first called in, and days passed before anyone began interviews. Weeks later, cadaver dogs alerted on a dorm room. Kristin was declared legally dead in 2002. Her body has never been found. For a quarter of a century, her family kept her name alive. Then a new sheriff reopened the file, investigators returned with ground-penetrating radar, cadaver dogs and soil testing, and a local musician's podcast found witnesses who had never spoken to police. After 26 years, a jury finally heard the case.

In this documentary: who Kristin was, the night she vanished, the failures of the first investigation, the podcast that found new witnesses, the searches, and the trial.
